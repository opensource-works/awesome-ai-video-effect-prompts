**Video prompt** ([source](https://x.com/AIwithkhan/status/2099707375943066094))

```text
Create a ultra-realistic cinematic fashion video using the uploaded images as exact visual references for the woman, crowd, wardrobe, framing, and atmosphere.
A young woman with the same short dark bob wears a fitted black turtleneck, black trousers, and black shoes, standing confidently at the center of a large crowd.
Start with her walking slowly toward the middle of the crowd, calm and expressionless, with a lit cigarette naturally held between her lips.
She reaches the center, stops, and faces directly into the camera.
She takes one slow drag from the cigarette, then removes it from her mouth.
She slowly exhales a visible stream of smoke toward the camera, with the smoke naturally drifting across her face.
She lowers her hand and becomes completely still, staring directly into the lens.
The entire crowd behind her remains frozen for a brief moment.
Suddenly, everyone behind her begins moving in perfect synchronization — sharp head turns, shoulder jerks, folding forward and snapping upright.
The movement should feel rhythmic, strange, powerful, and physically realistic.
The woman does not move at all while the entire crowd moves behind her.
Slowly push the camera closer to her, keeping her sharply focused while the crowd has subtle depth-of-field blur.
Dark, cold, mysterious lighting with deep shadows, realistic skin, hair, clothing, cigarette ember, and physically accurate smoke.
Photorealistic live-action quality, cinematic composition, natural human movement; no animation, CGI look, duplicated woman, distorted bodies, excessive smoke, text, subtitles, or logos.
```

### STORM trend: lead in white inside a crowd of black suits

<a href="https://x.com/mrdejie/status/2098606935222394948"><img src="https://assets.seadanse.com/seed-dance-2-5/ai-video-effect-prompts/x-2098606935222394948.jpg" width="460" alt="STORM trend: lead in white inside a crowd of black suits"></a>

A timed music-video shot: the lead smokes, walks into a formation of 40–60 men in black suits, turns to face the camera at the center and stands while they break into synchronized chest-clutching and bowing.

**Video and prompt by** [@mrdejie](https://x.com/mrdejie) · [original post](https://x.com/mrdejie/status/2098606935222394948) · Seedance 2.5 · 2026-09-12 · 38.7K views

**You bring:** Reference image of the lead (face, body, hair and outfit)

**Video prompt** ([source](https://x.com/mrdejie/status/2098611537074413882))

```text
Photoreal cinematic music-video shot, 30 seconds, 24fps, shallow-to-medium depth of field, overcast natural daylight, slight film grain. Use the provided reference image as the exact face, body, hair, and outfit of the lead. The lead is the only person wearing white.

**Lead character ( I use myself as the reference):**

**Background dancers:** 40–60 men of mixed East Asian and mixed ethnicities, various hair lengths.

---

**0.00–3.00s — Tight frontal close-up**  
Camera locked on the lead’s face and upper torso, eye-level, slight handheld micro-sway. He faces camera wearing black rectangular sunglasses. Cigarette in his mouth. He exhales a slow, thick cloud of white smoke that drifts across the lens. At 1.0s he raises his right hand (gold rings visible) and takes the cigarette between index and middle finger, bringing it to his lips for another short drag. At 2.2s he lowers the cigarette, still looking slightly down-camera with a frown. Background: out-of-focus wall of men in black suits standing motionless.

**3.00–4.20s — Head and body turn**  
He drops his gaze and turns his head and torso to his right (screen left), presenting the back of his head and the embroidered white jacket. Camera stays tight, following the turn.

**4.20–9.50s — Tracking from behind as he walks into the formation**  
Camera pulls back to a medium-wide over-the-shoulder / behind-the-back tracking shot. The lead walks slowly and steadily forward into the dense ranks of black-suited men. The men remain completely still, eyes forward or slightly down, like a living wall. Camera dollies backward at the same pace he walks, keeping him centered. We see more and more rows of black-suited men filling the frame. The brick building and arch become visible in the far background.

**9.50–13.80s — He threads through the crowd**  
He continues walking, now weaving between two columns. A few men shift half a step to let him pass but otherwise stay frozen. Camera continues tracking backward and slightly rising so we see more of the formation. By 13s he is deep inside the group.

**13.80–16.50s — He turns and settles in the middle**  
He stops, pivots 180 degrees so he now faces the camera again, standing in the exact center of the formation, one row back from the front line. He takes another drag, smoke curling. Camera has now become a locked wide shot looking slightly up the steps, the entire group framed symmetrically, brick arch behind them. He is the only figure in white; everyone else is black.

**16.50–20.80s — Stillness, then first twitch**  
Hold the wide composition. All black-suited men remain statue-still. The lead stands motionless except for the cigarette. At ~20.5s the men begin a tiny, almost invisible shift of weight.

**20.80–22.50s — Choreo ignition**  
On a sharp musical beat the entire black-suited group explodes into the same synchronized motion from the reference: they clutch their chests / ties with both hands, twist their torsos, and snap their heads in different directions. Movement is sharp, aggressive, and perfectly timed across the whole crowd. The lead does not participate; he stays upright, smoking, watching them.

**22.50–26.20s — Full collapse / bow wave**  
The dancers drop into a deep, chaotic-looking but actually tightly choreographed bow: they fold at the waist, heads whipping down then snapping back up, hair flying, some looking skyward with mouths open, others clutching their jackets. The motion travels through the ranks like a ripple. Camera remains the same wide locked-off shot so we see the whole staircase of bodies convulsing around the single still white figure in the center. He takes one more slow drag.

**26.20–30.00s — Hold and settle**  
The dancers continue the bowing / clutching / head-whip cycle two more times, then freeze in a mixed pose—some still bent, some half-risen, all looking strained.
```
